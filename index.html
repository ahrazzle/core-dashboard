What is CORE?

CORE is a dashboard for your car that runs in a web page. Plug a small Bluetooth adapter into your car's diagnostic port. The page reads the car's live data and shows it as gauges: speed, RPM, temperatures, fuel, and more. It also reads fault codes and can clear them.

The diagnostic port is the OBD-II port. Every car sold in the US since 1996 has one, usually under the dash on the driver's side.

The page talks to the adapter directly over Bluetooth. Nothing is sent to a server. There is no account and no app to install.

How to use it

  1. Get a Bluetooth Low Energy adapter. The box must say Bluetooth 4.0 or BLE. Older Bluetooth 2.0 adapters do not work with web pages.
  2. Plug it into the OBD-II port under your dash.
  3. Turn the ignition on. The engine can stay off for most readings.
  4. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on Android, Windows, or Mac.
  5. Tap Connect Bluetooth dongle and pick the adapter from the list.
  6. Drive. The gauges update live. Tap Read codes to check for fault codes.
  7. No adapter? Tap Try demo mode to explore with simulated data.

iPhone: Safari cannot use Bluetooth. Open this page in the free WebBLE app instead, or use demo mode. Looks: switch between Classic and Night Runners with the buttons at the top of the dashboard. The page remembers your choice.
